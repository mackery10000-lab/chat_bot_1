# Paper dialog artwork

These assets were generated with the built-in `image_gen` tool with `transparent_background: true`, inspected, and copied into the project. No CLI/API fallback was used. Text and controls remain live HTML. Dossier borders are sliced; ending certificates are displayed whole at their native 3:2 ratio so their locomotive seals cannot stretch.

## Dossier panel

Saved path: `public/assets/images/ui/dossier-panel.png`

Final prompt:

Use case: ui-mockup. Asset type: production raster background asset for an existing Korean sepia train mystery game named 13-Fri. Generate ONE blank antique railway investigation dossier panel, landscape 3:2 aspect ratio, front view perfectly flat and orthographic. Worn ivory archival paper mounted on a dark oxblood leather folder, narrow leather rim, thin distressed double ink border, subtle brass corner protectors, a small old brass paperclip on the top left edge. Very restrained stains and delicate natural paper fibers. Quiet pale cream middle, completely empty and clean across 86 percent of the area for live HTML Korean text, switches, and scrolling records; no ruled lines, no section dividers. Vintage 1930s railway detective game aesthetic, realistic illustrated materials, matching aged paper dialogue panels, dark clipboard inventory icons, walnut train interiors and amber brass. Crisp neat silhouette with subtly irregular paper edges, transparent exterior, fill almost entire canvas. No lettering, no symbols, no logos, no text, no baked controls, no drop shadow outside the panel. This is an actual reusable UI skin, not a screenshot or mockup of an application.

## Ending certificate

Saved path: `public/assets/images/ui/ending-certificate.png`

Final prompt:

Use case: ui-mockup. Asset type: production blank ending-card background for a sepia vintage railway mystery video game. Generate ONE landscape 3:2 antique railway last-journey certificate, front-facing flat orthographic, isolated on transparent background. A single warm ivory thick paper card, elegantly worn fine double-line border in faded umber, restrained dark oxblood leather corner at lower left and tarnished brass clip at upper right, subtle railway engraving flourishes at the four corners only. At the top center a SMALL faded circular ink stamp showing a steam locomotive silhouette without any text, occupying only 12 percent of card height. Remaining central 75 percent of card completely blank pale ivory, calm low-contrast paper fibers, space for live Korean ending title, one sentence, and restart button. Realistic illustrated game UI materials, aged cream paper, walnut brown and burgundy accents, solemn night-train atmosphere. Panel fills canvas with small transparent exterior margin, crisp edges, no outside shadow. No typography, no letters, no words, no labels, no numbers, no baked buttons, no scene, no characters. Actual reusable game UI skin, not an application screenshot.

## Sad ending certificate

Saved path: `public/assets/images/ui/ending-certificate-sad.png` (1536 × 1024).

Edit target: `ending-certificate.png`. Built-in tool, transparent output; original alpha and transparent holes preserved in the project PNG.

Final prompt specification:

Use case: precise-object-edit. Edit the provided blank landscape railway certificate into its SAD ENDING counterpart. Preserve the exact landscape 3:2 composition, four engraved railway corner flourishes, brass top-right clip and small circular centered locomotive seal. Keep the locomotive upright and undistorted. Make the paper deeply weathered, low-saturation ashen charcoal grey, with brittle creases, soot, frayed edges and three to five small irregular holes near corners and edges with true transparent alpha. The central 70 percent must remain empty, clean dark charcoal for bright live ivory HTML text. Use pale grey faded engravings and train stamp, and a tarnished grey clip. No text, letters, numbers, controls, skulls or background. A sorrowful, dignified counterpart to the warm happy-ending paper.

The happy and sad sheets are now native-ratio `<img>` elements, not sliced panel backgrounds. Live ending titles, descriptions and restart actions use the existing game font. The mechanical control device is excluded from the dossier skin and retains its original dark presentation.
